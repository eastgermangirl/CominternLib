Enver Hoxha

Letter of thanks to the Peruvian Communist Party (Marxist-Leninist)

Date: November 17, 1978
Source: Translated from Albanian text published in Bandera Roja, vol. XVII, no. 63, July 1979 (Peru), page 36.
Public Domain: Marxists Internet Archive (2023). You may freely copy, distribute, display and perform this translation; as well as make derivative and commercial works. Please credit “Marxists Internet Archive” as your source.

Central Committee

of the Peruvian Communist Party (M-L)

 

LIMA

 

Please accept my warm and fraternal thanks for the greetings you sent me on the occasion of my 70th birthday.

Taking advantage of this opportunity, I wish the communists, the working class and the Peruvian revolutionary peasantry, the Marxist-Leninist Communist Party, successes in the righteous struggle for national and regional liberation, against the forces of reaction, of American imperialism and Soviet social imperialism, and revisionists of all kinds.

I also hope that unity is strengthened even more between our two parties on the basis of Marxism-Leninism.

 

ENVER HOXHA

FIRST SECRETARY OF THE CENTRAL COMMITTEE

Party of Labor of ALBANIA

 

Tirana, November 17, 1978
